Support

Make photos and videos smaller on your iPhone.

Choose Photos or Videos, import a file, set a size limit, then compress. Review the result and save it to Photos, Files, or a destination in the iOS share sheet.

My file cannot reach the size I chose.

Try a larger size limit. For video, you can also trim the clip. Very small limits may reduce resolution and quality. Minufold shows the actual output size after processing.

A file will not import or finish processing.

Make sure the source file has finished downloading from iCloud or its file provider, and that your iPhone has enough available storage. Try opening the original in Photos or Files, then import it again. Photo compression supports still JPEG, PNG, and HEIC images. Animated and multi-frame images are currently unsupported.

How do I save or find a compressed file?

Open its completed record and choose a save or share action. Saving to Photos asks for permission to add files. If access was denied, allow it in iPhone Settings or save to Files. Exported copies remain in the destination you choose.

How do I remove a record?

Swipe left on a record, choose Delete, and confirm. Deleting a record removes its working copies from Minufold. Your original file in Photos or Files, and copies you previously exported, are managed separately in those apps.

How do Pro purchases and restoration work?

Pro offers a yearly auto-renewing subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Both unlock unlimited photo and video processing and batch compression. The current price and currency appear in the App Store purchase confirmation.

To restore access, use the Apple Account used for the purchase, open Minufold Settings, and choose Restore Purchases. An active yearly subscription or an eligible lifetime purchase can restore Pro access.

How do I cancel my yearly subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. A yearly subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Apple charges the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. After cancellation, access continues until the paid period expires.

A lifetime purchase does not automatically cancel an existing yearly subscription. If you switch to lifetime, manage the yearly subscription in Apple’s subscription settings. Deleting the App also does not cancel a subscription.

使用帮助

在 iPhone 上压缩图片和视频。

选择图片或视频区域，导入文件，设置大小上限，再开始压缩。查看结果后，可保存到相册、文件，或通过 iOS 分享面板发送。

文件无法压到我设置的大小

可以适当提高大小上限，视频也可以先裁短片段。过小的上限可能降低分辨率和画质。处理完成后，App 会显示结果文件的实际大小。

文件无法导入或压缩未完成

确认原文件已从 iCloud 或文件提供方下载完成，并确保 iPhone 有足够的可用空间。先在相册或文件 App 中打开原文件，再尝试导入。图片压缩支持静态 JPEG、PNG 和 HEIC，暂不支持动图及多帧图片。

如何保存和找到压缩结果

打开已完成的记录，选择保存或分享。保存到相册时会申请添加权限。如果此前拒绝了权限，可在 iPhone 设置中允许，或改为保存到文件。已导出的副本保存在你选择的位置。

如何删除记录

向左滑动记录，点击删除并确认。删除记录会清理 Minufold 内的工作副本。相册或文件中的原文件，以及之前导出的副本，由对应 App 单独管理。

Pro 有哪些购买方式，如何恢复

Pro 提供自动续订包年和一次性买断，两种方式均可解锁不限次数的图片、视频处理及批量压缩。实际价格与货币以 App Store 购买确认页为准。

恢复权益时，请使用原购买 Apple 账户，在 Minufold 设置中选择恢复购买。有效包年订阅或可恢复的买断购买可恢复 Pro 权益。

如何取消包年订阅

打开 iPhone 设置，点击你的姓名，再进入订阅。包年会自动续订，请至少在当前周期结束前 24 小时取消。Apple 会在周期结束前 24 小时内收取续订费用。取消后，可继续使用至已付费周期结束。

买断不会自动取消已有包年订阅。改为买断后，请在 Apple 订阅设置中管理原包年。删除 App 也不会取消订阅。
