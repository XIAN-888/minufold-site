Privacy policy

Updated November 11, 2026

Minufold is an iPhone photo and video compression app provided by Guoxin Xian. This policy explains how the App and support service handle information.

Selected media and local processing

Minufold imports files you choose through the system photo picker, Files, or the iOS share extension. Photo and video inspection, compression, trimming, and previews run on your device. The developer does not receive your media through the App.

The App stores working copies, compressed results, file names, media properties, processing history, successful export records, and preferences locally. The main App and its share extension use a shared on-device container to receive files. Minufold does not require an account.

Saving, sharing, and system services

Saving to Photos requests permission to add files. Sharing or exporting sends copies to the app or location you select in the system interface. That destination’s privacy practices apply to the exported copies.

iOS or a file provider may download your selected files from iCloud or another service. App data may be included in device or iCloud backups according to your system settings. Apple and the relevant provider manage those services.

Purchases

Apple processes payments, subscriptions, and purchase restoration. Minufold uses Apple StoreKit to check verified purchase information and determine Pro access for saving, sharing, and batch export. The developer does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password.

Data collection and tracking

Minufold has no advertising or developer-operated analytics. The App does not send your files, processing history, or preferences to a developer server, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Deleting local data

You can delete individual records or selected records in Minufold. This removes their local working copies and results. If a file cannot be removed immediately, the App reports the issue and retries cleanup when reopened. Original files in Photos or Files, exported copies, and system backups are managed separately in their respective apps or settings.

Removing the App deletes its local app data through iOS. To cancel a subscription, use Apple’s subscription settings.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond and investigate the request, retaining it while needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Contact and policy updates

Privacy questions can be sent to xiangguoxin520@icloud.com. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date.

隐私政策

更新日期为 2026 年 11 月 11 日

Minufold 是由 Guoxin Xian 提供的 iPhone 图片与视频压缩 App。本政策说明 App 和支持服务如何处理信息。

所选媒体与本地处理

Minufold 导入你通过系统照片选择器、文件或 iOS 分享扩展选择的文件。图片和视频的识别、压缩、裁剪及预览均在设备上完成。开发者不会通过 App 接收你的媒体文件。

App 在本地保存工作副本、压缩结果、文件名、媒体属性、处理记录、成功导出记录和偏好设置。主 App 与分享扩展通过设备内的共享容器接收文件。使用 Minufold 无需注册账户。

保存、分享与系统服务

保存到相册时，App 会申请添加文件的权限。分享或导出时，文件副本会发送到你在系统界面中选择的 App 或位置，导出后的副本适用对应目的地的隐私规则。

iOS 或文件提供方可能从 iCloud 或其他服务下载你选择的文件。根据系统设置，App 数据可能包含在设备或 iCloud 备份中。这些服务由 Apple 或相应提供方管理。

购买

付款、订阅及恢复购买由 Apple 处理。Minufold 通过 Apple StoreKit 核验购买信息，并确定保存、分享与批量导出的 Pro 权益。开发者不会接收你的银行卡信息或 Apple 账户密码。

数据收集与追踪

Minufold 没有广告或开发者运营的分析服务。App 不会将文件、处理记录或偏好设置发送到开发者服务器，也不会跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你。

删除本地数据

你可以在 Minufold 中删除单条或所选记录，清理对应的本地工作副本和结果。如果某个文件暂时无法删除，App 会显示提示，并在重新打开时重试清理。相册或文件中的原文件、导出的副本及系统备份，由各自的 App 或系统设置单独管理。

移除 App 时，iOS 会删除其本地 App 数据。取消订阅需前往 Apple 订阅设置操作。

支持邮件

如果你发送支持邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址及邮件中提供的信息。这些信息用于回复和调查问题，在处理支持请求或履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。你可以联系我们申请删除支持往来邮件。

联系与政策更新

隐私相关问题请发送至 xiangguoxin520@icloud.com。政策发生变更时，我们会在此页更新内容和日期。
